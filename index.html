Holy Bible – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Holy Bible Android application handles information when you use the app.

1. Information the App Uses

Holy Bible does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information to use its core Bible-reading features.

2. Bible Content and Local Data

Bible books, chapters, and verses may be included and stored locally within the application for reading and searching. Information stored locally on your device remains subject to the security and settings of your device.

3. Microphone and Voice Search

Holy Bible may request microphone permission when you choose to use voice search. Microphone access is used to support voice input and is not intended for continuous background recording.

Voice input may be processed by the Android/device speech-recognition service or another speech-recognition provider used by the app. Depending on the service used, spoken input may be transmitted to that service for speech recognition according to the provider's applicable privacy practices.

You can deny or revoke microphone permission through Android settings and continue using features that do not require microphone access.

4. AI Features

Holy Bible includes AI-powered Bible-related functionality. When you choose to use an AI feature, information contained in your request may be transmitted to and processed by the relevant Google/Firebase AI service to generate a response.

Please do not submit passwords, financial information, or other sensitive personal information through AI features.

5. Internet and Network Services

The app may use an internet connection for features that require online services, including AI functionality. Network communications use security mechanisms provided by the applicable platform and service.

6. Collection and Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to provide features that you choose to use, such as speech recognition and AI functionality, or when disclosure is required by applicable law.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Holy Bible does not require a user account. Information processed by third-party services may be retained or handled according to those services' own privacy policies and terms.

You may uninstall the app to remove locally stored application data, subject to Android backup and device behavior.

8. Children's Privacy

Holy Bible does not knowingly request personal information from children through the app.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Third-Party Services

Some features may rely on services provided by Google or other third parties. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies, terms, and security practices.

11. Your Choices

You can control Android permissions, including microphone access, through your device settings. You may also choose not to use voice-search or AI features that require network processing.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the Holy Bible app, please use the developer contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
